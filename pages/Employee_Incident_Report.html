Section 10 of 12 — Signed authorizations

Authorizations

Three release authorizations, typically required for a workers'-comp claim. Typing your name below and checking the box serves as your electronic signature. SSNs are omitted here and collected at the office.

Privacy Authorization (Amerisure)
I authorize any physician, surgeon, dentist, hospital, rehabilitation/convalescent/custodial facility, ambulance owner, nurse ("covered entities"), or insurance company to furnish to any representative of Amerisure Mutual Insurance Company / Amerisure Insurance Company all records in their possession regarding my injuries, medical history, and physical condition, both before and after the date of loss. This information will be used for verifying, evaluating, negotiating, and other pertinent legal purposes related to my claim for injuries. This authorization remains in force until my claim for injuries has been resolved and closed. I understand I have the right to revoke this authorization in writing at any time, with notice to the Company at PO Box 1515, Canonsburg, PA 15317, and that revocation is not effective to the extent the covered entities or insurance company have already relied on the use or disclosure of this information. I understand that information disclosed under this authorization may be subject to redisclosure by the recipient and may no longer be protected by federal or state law. This is not a release of claim for damages.
Authorization to Obtain Information
I authorize any licensed physician, medical practitioner, nurse, pharmacist, hospital, clinic, other medical-related facility, insurance company, or reinsurance company that has information as to my diagnosis, treatment, or prognosis of any physical or mental condition — including alcohol or drug abuse information — and any information regarding my occupation and salary, to give this information to the particular affiliated company of Builder's Bloc, my employer, or any insurance company, reinsurer, its claim management company, and its legal representatives to which I am submitting a claim. I understand this information will be used to determine eligibility for insurance benefits and will not be released except to persons or organizations performing a business or legal service connected with my claim, or as otherwise required by law. I may request a copy of this authorization at any time. This authorization is valid for the shortest of: the term of a health-insurance policy, the duration of any other claim, or five years from the date signed. By signing, I agree that authorized treating medical professionals may discuss my medical condition with my employer or their agents for adjudication and processing of my workers' compensation case. Note: under the Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act (GINA), medical providers should not include genetic information (including family medical history or genetic test results) in response to this authorization.
SSA Consent for Release of Information

Section 11 of 12

Drug & alcohol policy acknowledgment

Read the policy summary
Builder's Bloc maintains a Drug and Alcohol policy to protect the safety of all employees. Illegal drug use, possession, or a positive test at work can result in discharge. Alcohol use or impairment on the job, or a blood-alcohol level of 0.04% or more while on Company premises or operating a Company vehicle, can also result in discharge. The Company may require random or post-accident drug and alcohol testing as permitted by law. Refusing a post-accident test when there is sufficient cause, or when policy authorizes post-injury testing, can result in forfeiture of benefits. Full policy details are available from the office on request.
