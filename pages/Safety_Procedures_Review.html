Builder's Bloc Safety Procedures Review

General Site Conditions

  • A site evaluation must be done on a daily basis prior to commencing any work. If you notice any damage or any unsafe conditions in or to any part of a home you must notify your company's supervisor and a McBride representative prior to commencing work.
  • Tools must be checked daily to ensure that all safety mechanisms are in good working order. Any tool that is found not to be in good working order MUST BE TAKEN OUT OF SERVICE. This includes cords, ladders, saws, nail guns, etc.
  • PPE must be worn at all times. This includes: hard hats, safety glasses, shirts, pants, and work boots.
  • Walkways must be kept clear of any obstructions (i.e. hoses, cords, and debris).
  • Ramps must be built at any entrances with a step over 18 inches high into the building. All ramps must consist of at least 2 – 2″ x 10″ boards with cleats.
  • Keep the saw cutting area clear. Do not let cut-off material pile up.
  • Controlled Access Zones must be utilized. Make sure CAZ is clearly posted on at all entrances to a building; lines marked and unauthorized personnel kept away from the danger zone.
  • First Aid Kits must be at each jobsite or lot and every crew member must know where it is located (in a vault is not acceptable).
  • Fire extinguishers must be with each crew and in working order.
  • Stair holes must remain covered and clearly marked “HOLE” if not surrounded by guard rails. All exterior wall openings must be railed off with top and bottom rails.
  • Heat holes must be covered with plywood plugs.
  • If furnaces are activated during construction, plywood covers must be replaced with temporary vents or other acceptable devices.
  • Jobsites must be picked up and kept in an orderly basis at all times. Waiting until the job is finished to clean up creates a hazardous worksite and is unacceptable.
  • Do not walk on concrete with caution tape around it. Only a representative from the general contractor or the flatwork company can remove caution tape. You will be held personally responsible for damaging concrete.
  • Do not bring unauthorized tools onto the jobsite.
  • Only use approved manufactured electrical devices. No homemade plugs, computer surge protectors, etc.
  • No smoking inside of buildings.

Nail Gun Usage

  • Make sure all safety features of your nail gun are working. Only use nail guns with the sequential trigger.
  • Only nail forward, never backward.
  • Be aware of your surroundings. Make sure you or anyone around you is out of the way and cannot be hit by a misdirected nail.
  • Never carry a nail gun with your finger on the trigger.
  • Always disconnect the nail gun or any power tool from its power source before working on it.

Fall Protection

  • Floor joists are to be set into place such that a platform is created, thus allowing each joist to be rolled into place. Only competent persons that have been trained are allowed to perform this task.
  • Controlled Decking Zones must be created and identified by an orange line located 6 feet inside the perimeter of each home. Only qualified workers are allowed to perform tasks inside this zone until exterior walls are erected and secured.
  • Trusses must be installed using ladders or some type of scaffold device when working on the exterior wall. Workers are permitted to work from the bottom chord to place and secure trusses when working from an interior position.
  • In situations where this may not be feasible, such as two-story great rooms, the supervisor may develop and document an alternative fall protection plan using Builder's Bloc's fall protection guidelines.
  • WORKERS WITHIN 6′ OF THE EXTERIOR WALL ARE NOT ALLOWED TO WORK ABOVE THE TOP PLATE WITHOUT A PERSONAL FALL ARREST SYSTEM.
  • The first row of sheathing and slide arrestor must be installed from an interior position.
  • All roof pitches 8/12 and above must have additional slide arrestors installed as necessary.
  • All extension ladders must extend 36″ or 3 rungs when being used to access an elevated work surface.
  • Properly secure all ladders prior to usage.
  • Workers performing siding duties with pump jack style scaffold systems must use fall arrestor systems (yo-yo's or ropes) at all heights.
  • Scaffolds may never be moved while they are in use by a worker.
