Someone Got Hurt: What To Do

🏠 Home

Follow these steps in order for any injury on the job, no matter how small.

1

Is it an emergency? Call 911 first.

Call 911 right away for any of these. When in doubt, call.

  • Not breathing, or unconscious
  • Chest pain or signs of a stroke
  • Bleeding that will not stop
  • A fall from height, or a possible head, neck or back injury
  • An amputation or crushed limb
  • A serious burn or an electrical shock
  • Confusion, collapse or a seizure
  • Trouble breathing or a severe allergic reaction

📞 Call 911

Tell 911 the subdivision, street and lot number, and send someone to the entrance to wave them in. Do not move the injured person unless they are in danger where they are. Give first aid while you wait: see the First Aid Quick Reference.

2

Call Laura Weppner-Herndon.

📞 Call Laura: 314-581-6841

  • If 911 is not needed, call Laura first, before anything else.
  • If you called 911, call Laura next.
  • Laura directs the care. She tells you where to go and sets up the appointment.
3

Call your superintendent.

Let them know what happened and where.

4

Do not let the injured worker leave alone.

  • The injured worker is not allowed to leave the job site on their own.
  • They must be escorted by a trusted Builder's Bloc employee, in the same vehicle.
5

If the worker declines treatment.

  • They must sign the Emergency Medical Refusal of Care form THAT DAY.
  • Declining treatment also means declining the drug test. The drug screening box must be checked on the form.
  • A refusal of care forfeits all future eligibility for treatment by the company for that injury.
  • Under Missouri workers' compensation law (RSMo 287.120.6), refusing a drug or alcohol test requested by the employer results in the forfeiture of benefits.
6

Fill out the reports.

Once the worker is taken care of, each person completes their own form:

  • Injured employee: Employee Incident / Injury Report
  • Crew foreman: Crew Foreman Statement
  • Anyone who saw it: Incident Witness Statement
  • Superintendent: Superintendent Investigation

Before the scene is cleaned up, take photos and leave any tools or equipment involved where they are.
