On the Job Stretches

Always consult with your physician or other qualified health care provider before embarking on a new treatment, diet, or fitness program.

Pillar Stretch

Stand straight with your natural inward curve at the low back (power tuck). Interlock fingers and keep elbows straight. Raise arms overhead, pushing them back behind your head. Hold this position for a 10-second count. Repeat 2 to 3 times.

Levator Scapula Stretch

Sitting or standing in a balanced posture with your ears over your shoulders: 1. Tilt your head to the right side. Then 2. turn your head to the right side. Hold 10 seconds. Repeat 2 to 3 times to each side.

Chin Tuck

Without lifting your chin up or down, slowly guide your head straight back. Hold for 5 seconds. Repeat 5 times.

Forearm (Wrist Flexors) Stretch

Straighten right arm in front of you with your palm up. Extend hand down. Gently push down with left hand. Hold for 10 seconds. Repeat 2 to 3 times. Repeat for your opposite hand.

Shoulder Stretch

Stand straight with your natural inward curve at the low back (power tuck). Reach across your body with your left arm. Grab the left elbow with your right hand and gently pull across your body. Hold 10 seconds. Repeat 2 to 3 times to each side.

Chicken Wing

Stand straight with your natural inward curve at the low back (power tuck). Place your right palm at the base of your neck. Grasp your right elbow with your left hand and pull your elbow behind your head. As you do that motion, lean your trunk to the left. Repeat this exercise with the left elbow and lean to your right. Hold this position for a 10-second count. Repeat 2 to 3 times to each side.

Quadriceps Stretch

Stand straight with your natural inward curve at the low back (power tuck). Face a truck or wall, placing one hand on the surface for balance. Bend your opposite heel to your buttocks. Pull heel to your buttocks with your hand. Hold this position for a 10-second count. Repeat 2 to 3 times to each side.

Standing Hamstring Stretch

Stand straight with one foot on a step, a stool, or a chair. Keep your legs straight. Keep your back straight. Lean forward gently, bending at the hip; do not bend at the waist. Hold for 10 seconds. Repeat 2 to 3 times each side.

Standing Extension

Stand straight with both hands on your hips. Gently lean back at the waist. Keep your head level and your knees straight. Hold for 5 seconds. Repeat 10 times.
