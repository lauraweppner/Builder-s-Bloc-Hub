Formula #38 Drywall & Metal Framing Adhesive
Henkel Consumer Adhesives (Pro-Series) · Solvent-based construction adhesive
FlammableSolvent vapors

Also covers: Drywall construction adhesive

⚠️ Hazards

  • FLAMMABLE. Flash point 21°F. Vapors are heavier than air, travel along the ground, and can ignite explosively.
  • Closed containers may rupture in extreme heat.
  • Vapors can cause headache, drowsiness, dizziness and breathing irritation.
  • Irritates eyes. Repeated skin contact dries skin and can cause dermatitis.
  • Long, repeated overexposure to solvents has been linked to brain, nervous system, liver and kidney damage. Deliberately inhaling vapors may be harmful or fatal.
  • Contains heptane, petroleum naphtha and xylene.

🥽 Protective gear

  • Chemical goggles or safety glasses.
  • Rubber gloves where skin contact is likely; impervious clothing and shoes.
  • Ventilate during application and curing. Air-supplied respirator if limits are exceeded and ventilation is not adequate.

🩹 First aid

EyesFlush gently with water for at least 15 minutes, lifting the eyelids. Get prompt medical attention.
SkinWash thoroughly with soap and water. Remove contaminated clothing.
BreathingMove to fresh air. If breathing is difficult, give oxygen; if it has stopped, give artificial respiration. Get medical attention.
SwallowedDO NOT induce vomiting. Get immediate medical attention.

🔥 Fire

  • Use carbon dioxide, dry chemical or foam. Use water fog to cool nearby material.
  • Firefighters need self-contained breathing apparatus and full protective gear.

🧹 Spill and clean-up

  • Eliminate all ignition sources. Ventilate (open windows and doors).
  • Wear protective clothing. Cover with absorbent and scoop into a sealable safety container.

📦 Handling and storage

  • Keep away from heat, sparks and flame.
  • Keep the container closed when not in use. Use only in a well-ventilated area.
  • Do not cut or burn empty containers. Do not reuse the container.

Galvanized Sheet Steel (Metal Studs)WARNING
United States Steel Corporation · Galvanized steel
Cancer hazard labelFume if cut or welded

Also covers: Galvanized (hot dipped) sheet, galvannealed sheet, ACRYZINC sheet, carbon steel. Listed in the inventory as Metal Studs.

⚠️ Hazards

  • Little or no hazard as a solid. Welding, burning, cutting, sawing or grinding makes dust and fume that can damage lungs and other organs.
  • Zinc oxide fume from galvanized steel can cause "metal fume fever": flu-like symptoms lasting 12 to 48 hours.
  • Cancer hazard label: the coating may contain small amounts of lead and/or nickel, and some product has a hexavalent chromium surface treatment.
  • Coatings and oils may irritate skin with prolonged contact.

🥽 Protective gear

  • Safety glasses or goggles for cutting, sawing, grinding or welding. Do not wear contact lenses.
  • Gloves when handling coated product and for welding or burning.
  • NIOSH-approved respirator if ventilation does not control dust and fume.

🩹 First aid

EyesFlush with large amounts of clean water to remove particles. Get medical attention if irritation persists.
SkinRemove contaminated clothing. Wash with soap and water. For a burn, flush with cold water and get medical attention.
BreathingMove to fresh air. If breathing is difficult or has stopped, give artificial respiration or oxygen. Get medical attention promptly.
SwallowedNot likely. If it happens, get medical attention immediately.

🔥 Fire

  • Does not burn. Use an extinguisher that suits the surrounding materials.
  • Do not use water on molten metal.

🧹 Spill and clean-up

  • Solid steel: not applicable.
  • For fine dust: vacuum or wet sweep. Do not use compressed air. Keep out of sewers and waterways.

📦 Handling and storage

  • Store away from acids.
  • Use ventilation when cutting, welding or grinding. Avoid breathing dust and fume.

Gypsum Board
Temple-Inland · Gypsum board (drywall)
DustSilica

Also covers: Gypsum wallboard, fire rated board, foil back wallboard, water resistant wallboard, gypsum sheathing, coreboard, veneer plaster base, shaft liner

⚠️ Hazards

  • NIOSH-approved respirator when snapping or sawing makes dust; required if dust is over the OSHA limit.

SkinFlush with fresh water for at least 15 minutes. If irritation persists, see a physician.

🔥 Fire

  • Paper surface could burn. Use water spray or carbon dioxide.

🧹 Spill and clean-up

  • Landfill in line with local, state and federal rules. Not a hazardous waste.

📦 Handling and storage

  • Avoid high humidity.
  • Score and snap instead of sawing to keep dust down.
  • Use local exhaust or dust collection when sanding or sawing.

Gypsum Wallboard Panels
American Gypsum · Gypsum board (drywall)
DustSilicaHeavy panels

Also covers: EagleRoc, FireBloc Type X and Type C, AquaBloc, M-Bloc, SmoothRoc, exterior sheathing, soffit board, ceiling board, shaft liner, veneer plaster base

⚠️ Hazards

  • Dust from cutting, sawing or sanding can irritate eyes, skin, nose, throat and lungs.
  • May contain a small amount of crystalline silica. Breathing that dust over a long time can cause lung disease (silicosis); silica is classed as a cancer hazard.
  • Panels are heavy and can fall over, causing serious injury or death.

🥽 Protective gear

  • Safety glasses with side shields or goggles. Do not wear contact lenses in dusty areas.
  • NIOSH-approved respirator when cutting or sawing makes dust.
  • Gloves or protective clothing are usually not needed but may be wanted in some situations.

🩹 First aid

EyesRemove contact lenses. Flush thoroughly with water, including under the eyelids. Call a physician immediately.
SkinWash gently with soap and water.
BreathingMove to fresh air. Get medical help if coughing or other symptoms do not go away.
SwallowedNot applicable for the product as supplied.

🔥 Fire

  • Not combustible.
  • Self-contained breathing apparatus is recommended when fighting any fire.

🧹 Spill and clean-up

  • Not a hazardous waste. Dispose of in line with local, state and federal rules.

📦 Handling and storage

  • Keep dry. Stack or store panels flat.
  • Do not store above 125°F for long periods or in high humidity.
  • Score and snap instead of sawing to keep dust down.
  • Use proper lifting methods and equipment.

Sandpaper (Coated Abrasive Products)
Grabber · Sandpaper
Sanding dustNoise

Also covers: Aluminum oxide, silicon carbide or alumina zirconia abrasive on paper, cloth or fiber

⚠️ Hazards

  • Sanding dust may cause coughing and shortness of breath; long-term it may affect breathing capacity.
  • Dust may irritate eyes. The abrasive can scrape skin.
  • Sanding some materials is loud enough to affect hearing.
  • Most of the dust comes from the material being sanded, not the sandpaper.

🥽 Protective gear

  • Approved dust respirator (required).
  • Eye protection (required).
  • Hearing protection.
  • Gloves as desired.

🩹 First aid

EyesWash with a large amount of water. Get medical help if needed.
SkinGet first aid or medical help if needed.
BreathingMove to fresh air. Give artificial respiration if needed. Get first aid or medical help.
SwallowedNo known harmful effects. Get first aid or medical help if needed.

🔥 Fire

  • Backing and resin binder will burn or break down. Any fire-fighting material can be used; use respiratory protection.

🧹 Spill and clean-up

  • Normal clean-up. Vacuum dust.

📦 Handling and storage

  • Handle with adequate ventilation. Use dust collection with proper filtration.

SHEETROCK Brand Gypsum Panels, FIRECODE Core, Type XWARNING
United States Gypsum Company (USG) · Fire-rated gypsum board
DustSilicaHeavy panels

Also covers: Type X gypsum board

⚠️ Hazards

  • High dust levels may irritate skin, eyes, nose, throat and upper airways.
  • Frequent contact can dry the skin and may lead to dermatitis.
  • Contains under 1% crystalline silica and under 1% glass fiber. Long, repeated breathing of respirable silica dust can cause lung disease (silicosis) and lung cancer.
  • Panels are heavy and can fall over, causing serious injury or death.

🥽 Protective gear

  • Safety glasses or goggles.
  • NIOSH/MSHA-approved particulate respirator when dusty, in poorly ventilated areas, or if exposure limits are exceeded.
  • Gloves and protective clothing for repeated or prolonged contact (chemical gloves are not needed).

🩹 First aid

EyesFlush thoroughly with water for 15 minutes to remove particles. If irritation persists, see a physician.
SkinWash with mild soap and water. Hand lotion can help dry skin. If irritation persists, see a physician.
BreathingMove to fresh air and stay away from the dust until coughing and other symptoms stop. Contact a physician if conditions warrant.
SwallowedNo harmful effects expected, but a large amount could block the gut. If stomach upset occurs, call a physician.

🔥 Fire

  • Not expected to burn.
  • Use water or whatever suits the surrounding fire.

🧹 Spill and clean-up

  • Shovel or sweep up dry material; avoid making dust.
  • Use a HEPA-filtered industrial vacuum, or a dust suppressant if sweeping. Do not use compressed air.
  • Washing it down may plug drains. Never discharge large releases into sewers or surface water.

📦 Handling and storage

  • Store cool, dry and ventilated, protected from weather and sustained moisture.
  • Store flat where possible. If stacked on edge, keep 4 to 6 inches from the wall.
  • Do not create a tripping hazard or exceed floor load limits.
  • Use proper lifting techniques; forklifts must be rated for the load.

SHEETROCK FIRECODE CompoundWARNING
United States Gypsum Company (USG) · Setting-type joint compound (powder)
Burn hazardSanding dustSilica

Also covers: Joint compound, taping compound, mud (powder, plaster of paris based)

⚠️ Hazards

  • Dust may irritate eyes, skin, nose, throat and upper airways.
  • Long, repeated breathing of respirable mica dust may cause lung disease (pneumoconiosis).
  • Contains under 5% crystalline silica. Long, repeated breathing of respirable silica dust can cause lung disease (silicosis) and lung cancer.

🥽 Protective gear

  • Safety glasses or goggles.
  • NIOSH/MSHA-approved particulate respirator when dusty, in poorly ventilated areas, or if exposure limits are exceeded.
  • Gloves and protective clothing to prevent repeated or prolonged skin contact.

🩹 First aid

EyesDo not rub or scratch. Flush thoroughly with water for 15 minutes. If irritation persists, see a physician.
SkinWash with mild soap and water (plaster dries the skin). Hand lotion can help. If irritation persists, see a physician.
BreathingMove to fresh air and stay away until coughing and other symptoms stop. Contact a physician if conditions warrant.
SwallowedPlaster of paris hardens and may block the gut. Drinking gelatin solution or large amounts of water may delay setting. Call a physician.

🔥 Fire

  • Not expected to burn.
  • Use water or whatever suits the surrounding fire.

🧹 Spill and clean-up

  • Normal clean-up. No special precautions.
  • Never discharge large releases into sewers or surface water.

📦 Handling and storage

  • Store cool, dry and ventilated. Any moisture will harden the powder in storage.
  • Wet-sand to cut down dust.

Ivaco Rolling Mills Limited Partnership · Steel
Fume if cut or weldedPoor scan

Also covers: Hand-labeled "Simpson Tie Steel Rod/Wire" in the book; listed in the inventory as Simpson Tie Steel Nail.

🧹 Spill and clean-up

  • Can be recycled or sent to a permitted landfill.

📦 Handling and storage

  • Use lifting equipment within its rated capacity.
