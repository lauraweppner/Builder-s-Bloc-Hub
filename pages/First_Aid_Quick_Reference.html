First Aid Quick Reference

Illness and injury signs, symptoms and first aid.

Illness / InjurySigns / SymptomsFirst Aid
Stroke
  • F Face – facial droop or uneven smile
  • A Arm – arm numbness or weakness
  • S Speech – slurred speech, difficulty speaking
  • T Time – call 911 and get to hospital immediately
 CALL 911 IMMEDIATELY!
Heart Attack / Chest Pain
  • Chest discomfort that feels like pressure, fullness, or squeezing pain lasting more than a few minutes or goes away and returns
  • Pain and discomfort to other parts of your upper body, like one or both arms, or your back, neck, stomach, teeth, and jaw
  • Unexplained shortness of breath, with or without chest discomfort
  • Cold sweats
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Lightheadedness
  • Anxiety, indigestion
  • Unexplained fatigue
 CALL 911 IMMEDIATELY!
Heat Stroke Hot dry flushed skin, no sweating, agitation and confusion, headache, nausea, vomiting, irregular pulse, rapid shallow breathing, seizure, loss of consciousness, possible cardiac arrest CALL 911
If conscious, use the heat exhaustion steps below.
If unconscious, clear airway and give CPR.
Heat Exhaustion Pale moist skin, cramps, headache or light-headedness, nausea, rapid pulse, shallow breathing, muscle weakness Move person to cool place, loosen outer clothing. Give fluids with electrolytes every 15 minutes as tolerated, spray with water, apply ice packs to armpits and groin.
Diabetic Related Emergency Hypoglycemia (low blood sugar): Shaky and weak, pale moist skin, nausea, confusion, irritable and behaving irrationally, palpitations, sweating. Sit person down, give sugar if fully conscious and alert (sugary drink, juice, etc.). Check response and seek medical advice. If allowable, after 10 minutes, give carbohydrate food (sandwich, crackers, cereal bar, etc.).
If loss of consciousness, CALL 911
Hyperglycemia (high blood sugar): Symptoms develop over time and include extreme thirst and frequent urination. In later stages, drowsy, which could lead to unconsciousness. If person collapses and you suspect hyperglycemia (fruity breath, check for bracelet), open airway and check breathing.
CALL 911!
If breathing, place on side, causing airway to open so fluid and/or vomit can drain. Check and note level of consciousness, breathing and pulse until help arrives.
