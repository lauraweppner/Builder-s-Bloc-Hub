First Aid Report

For injuries that are first aid only (not an OSHA recordable). Takes about 2 minutes. Your entries save on this device until you send.

Call Laura first for every injury, even a small one. She directs all care. Follow the same reporting steps as any injury.
Use this short form when the injury is first aid only, meaning it is not an OSHA recordable. A doctor or clinic visit can still be first aid, for example an exam, an X-ray, butterfly strips, or a non-prescription medicine. If it turns into recordable treatment (sutures or staples, a prescription, a rigid splint, physical therapy, restricted duty, or days away), use the full incident report set instead.
